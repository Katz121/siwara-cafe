Know the cityTakua Pa: The Town That Moved with the River

From an ancient settlement on the trans-peninsular route to a market and mining town · Verified facts and questions waiting to be answered.

6 minutesCity topicsAphon · The old town opens through clothing

The first of the six A's in Takua Pa old town · Kebaya, patterns, and Peranakan beauty through Chang Pong's brush

5 minutesOld houses and architectureBuildings built with tin money and dredger steel · Architecture that remembers the town

Read Takua Pa through the shophouses on Culture Street, Boon Sung Iron Bridge, the governor's residence, and Tao Ming School · The transition embedded in brick, mortar, and steel structures

6 minutesWaterways and tradeFrom Thung Tuk to the Takua Pa River: The Day the Waterway Built the Town

The river is not just a backdrop. It is a route, a connector of people, and the determiner of the town's fate, which had to relocate due to sedimentation.

7 minutesPeople and flavorsFlavours of the Baba Community and the Old Market

Food serves as both household memories and evidence of cultural exchange · Read Takua Pa through the Baba cuisine, double-layered Tao Sor, and the Culture Street

A creative space project that revived houses number 78, 109, and 305 · What the new generation tells about the town, and what history still needs to uncover
