Takua Pa Old Town Walking Leaflet

Two-sided, printed on A4, folded in half and ready to carry on your walk

This leaflet tells the story of a teak house in the old market district, then invites you to continue walking to four more places in town. It is made for people who want to walk without looking down at their phone all the time.
