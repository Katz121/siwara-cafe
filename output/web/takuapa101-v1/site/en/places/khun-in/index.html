Then

The district chief's house in a tin port town

Around 1917 (B.E. 2460); tenure context: 1921 to 1926 (B.E. 2464 to 2469)

Verified

หลักฐานท้องถิ่นระบุว่าบ้านขุนอินทร์สร้างขึ้นราว 1917 (B.E. 2460) โดยช่างฝีมือชาวจีน เพื่อเป็นบ้านพักของ ร.อ.ท. ขุนอินทรคีรี (ช้อย ณ นคร) ผู้สืบสายตระกูลเจ้าเมืองและต่อมาเป็นนายอำเภอTakua Paระหว่าง 1921 (B.E. 2464) ถึง 2469 จึงไม่ใช่เพียงอาคารสวยงาม แต่เป็นบ้านของผู้มีบทบาทในโครงสร้างการปกครองเมืองช่วงปลายยุคเจ้าเมืองสู่ระบบอำเภอ

The house emerged in Takua Pa as the town grew through trade and tin mining. Rivers and canals served as transport routes, trading boats from China and Burma exchanged goods, and many Thai people of Chinese and Hokkien Chinese descent settled here. Its two-storey adapted Sino-Portuguese brick-and-mortar architecture reflects both the owner's status and the Andaman trading world that connected Takua Pa with people of many cultures.

At that time, the house served as a residence for a senior official and formed part of an old-town landscape of merchant homes, shops, and religious sites. However, no primary document has been found that directly records its construction date, the craftsmen's names, or any other public function of the house.

Before

From a family home to an old town heritage

1981 (พ.ศ. 2524) to before 2026 (พ.ศ. 2569)

Verified

After several decades of use, the building deteriorated. The Na Nakhon family repaired it around 1981 (พ.ศ. 2524), largely retaining its original form and replacing turtle-back roof tiles with corrugated tiles. Among accessible sources, this is the conservation event for Khun In House with the clearest year.

Meanwhile, Takua Pa changed from a mining and water-trade centre into a quieter town as its former economy declined. Rivers and canals became shallow due to mining activity, and the old community gradually took on a cultural and tourism role. Khun In House is therefore remembered alongside the cultural street, Chinese-European buildings, and evidence of the former mining town.

ONEP's official sources classify Khun In House as an architectural art site, while the SAC community database lists it among Takua Pa's historical sites. However, a review of accessible sources found no year confirming registration as a Fine Arts Department historic site, no record of use for another business, and no evidence that the house was damaged by the 2004 tsunami (พ.ศ. 2547).

Now

A heritage building still under family care

2569 / 2026

Verified

The status verified in 2026 (พ.ศ. 2569) is that Khun In House continues to be described as a historic building in Takua Pa old town. Local sources state that it remains under the care of the Na Nakhon family. The building's value lies in its adapted Sino-Portuguese style and its historical link to Khun Intharakhiri.

The house has no public information verifying opening hours, admission fees, an on-site caretaker, or official access to the interior. It should therefore be treated as a building that can be viewed in its external context, while interior access remains unknown. It should not be described as a museum or a regular event venue.

Today, the house has a symbolic role in the old-town landscape, alongside the cultural road and other historic community buildings. Unlike projects with clear announcements, no news or announcement from 2022 to 2026 (B.E. 2565 to 2569) has been found that identifies restoration or activities specifically for Ban Khun In.

FAQ

What is the history of this place?

It was the residence of Captain Khun Intharakhiri (Choi Na Nakhon). Community tourism sources classify Khun In House among Takua Pa's historic sites and old buildings.

What are the highlights to see?

Its two-storey form is visible from outside. Its adapted Sino-Portuguese style blends Western and Eastern art, and it was the residence of a notable person named in the ONEP database.

How the municipality document mentions this place

A house described in documents as having Sino-European architecture and reflecting past prosperity
