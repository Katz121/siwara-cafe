Then

Starting year of the open-air market is not verified yet

Not verified

The available online evidence does not yet state when the open-air market was first built, who built it, or which project it began as. It is therefore not yet possible to responsibly say that this place originated in the tin-mining era, the era of the provincial rulers, or the period of modern urban expansion.

Takua Pa's context only tells us that the town has roots in trade and mining, and that the market district is an important community area. There is still no direct evidence linking this food center to a specific role in the town during those periods. Replacing the site's history with the history of the old town therefore risks creating a story that goes beyond the sources.

Before

A food market that gradually became the town's dining spot

At least 2020 to 2025

Verified

A food-review source published in 2020 (B.E. 2563) identifies Ko Ek's shop as being “in Takua Pa Open-Air Market” and records it as open during the daytime. This confirms that the area was already functioning as a market with food shops at that time, not merely as an empty lot.

In 2024 (B.E. 2567), Takua Pa Town Municipality promoted the open-air market as a center for many types of food, from rice with curry, made-to-order dishes, noodles, and khanom chin to snacks and drinks. A 2025 news report discusses plans to develop it into a modern food center or food court. This is a development plan, not evidence that construction was completed. No evidence has been found that the market was affected by the 2004 tsunami, abandoned, or registered as an ancient monument.

References · Aroi Jung Phang Nga · Khao Tom Haeng at Ko X shop, Lan Long Market, Takua Pa · เทศบาลเมืองตะกั่วป่า (Takua Pa Town Municipality) · Lan Long Market introduction video · Siam Rath · Lan Long Market development guidelines news
Now

Still a food hub, but the status of the new project requires following up

2569 / 2026

Conflicting information

The latest evidence found confirms that Lan Loeng Market existed during 2024 to 2025 (B.E. 2567 to 2568) and indicates that small vendors were still using the area to sell food. The municipality previously gave approximate opening hours of 08:00 to 22:00, but review platforms list a different schedule, so the current hours should not be treated as verified.

No 2026 (B.E. 2569) announcement has yet been found confirming the direct administrator, building condition, facilities, or opening to visitors as a tourist attraction. There is also no confirming evidence that the plan to upgrade it into a modern food center/food court has been completed. The safest overall description is that it is a food market used by the community and small vendors, with a development direction, while current details should still be checked again with the municipality.

What is the history of this place?

A promotional video from Takua Pa Municipality (Takua Pa Municipality), published on 1 September 2024 (B.E. 2567), presents Lan Loeng Market as a place offering many types of food. This evidence confirms promotion in that year, not the year the market was established. A report dated 19 June 2025 (B.E. 2568) discusses plans to develop Lan Loeng Market into a modern food court and add more street-food options. It reports a development plan, not evidence that the project was completed

What are the highlights to see?

The municipality recommends rice with curry and made-to-order dishes. These two types of noodle dishes appear in the food list promoted by the municipality. The municipality also says that snacks, desserts, and drinks are available in the market
