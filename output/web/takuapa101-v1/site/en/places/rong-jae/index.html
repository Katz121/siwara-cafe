Then

An old shrine of the Chinese community in Takua Pa

The exact construction year is unknown; municipal documents state that it is more than a century old

Verified

Rong Jae Nai Suan, or Seng Leng Keng Shrine, appears in Takua Pa Town Municipality documents as a centuries-old shrine, but the same documents do not give its construction year. It is therefore not yet possible to place its beginnings in a particular reign.

The founder identified in municipal documents is Mr So Thian Seng, an overseas Chinese settler. After the shrine was completed, Phra Cheng Chui Cho Sue, or Chao Pho Yat Nam Khang, was brought from China and enshrined there. The place therefore likely served both as a site of worship and as a centre of belief for the Chinese community in the mining and port town of Takua Pa. However, there is no site-specific evidence for its construction year, the founder's policy rationale, or its everyday role at the time.

Another local document says that Mr So Seng had worked with Ku Chai Tung Shrine before opening the shrine in the garden. However, it cannot yet be confirmed that he was the same person as Mr So Thian Seng, so these are not presented as the same fact.

Now

Still named in the Vegetarian Festival landscape, but operational status is unclear

2569 / 2026

Not verified

Currently, verifiable online sources do not confirm who manages the shrine, whether it is open to visitors every day, what condition the building is in, or whether any restoration project is under way. It should therefore not be described as an open tourist attraction or as a place that can be visited without an appointment.

The contemporary evidence found is a review article reporting the sale of ko sui sweets at Rong Jae Nai Suan during the Vegetarian Festival. This indicates that the place was still used for a community festival activity, but it is a blog report, not a shrine schedule announcement, and does not confirm that the activity is held every year.

Travellers should use the Soi Rong Phra, Thanon Rat Bamrung address as a starting point and check with local people or the municipality before travelling, because no verified coordinates, opening hours, telephone number, or official contact channel are available.

What is the history of this place?

What are the highlights to see?
