TAKUA PA / PEOPLE & PLACES

A guide from Baan Siwara

Takuapa 101 was created by Baan Siwara in Takuapa to bring together places, traditions, and shops in the town for readers to explore in sequence

Siwara Cafe is a teak house in the old market district, recognizable by its wooden gable, colored glass, and traditional old wooden doors. It stands at 53 Rat Bamrung Road. We hope this wooden house will help people get to know the town more deeply.
